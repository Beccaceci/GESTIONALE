## 💡 Why This Repository Exists

> ### *Knowledge should belong to everyone, not just those who can afford it.*

The primary driving force behind this project is a firm belief that **education and high-quality study materials must be free, open, and accessible to everyone without barriers**. 

In this environment:
- **Selling lecture notes is unacceptable.** Nobody should ever have to pay for a fellow student's notes, summaries, or exam materials.
- **Equal opportunity in learning.** Every student, regardless of their background or personal circumstances, deserves access to clear, rigorous, and beautifully formatted resources to prepare for their exams.

This repository represents my concrete commitment to this philosophy: offering lecture notes that anyone can download, read, and learn from for free.

---

## 🤝 Building a Complete Academic Hub Together

Currently, this repository contains notes covering **only the first-year courses** (`courses/first_year/`).

However, **the long-term vision is much bigger**:

> **The Goal:** Create a single, centralized, and freely accessible repository covering **every single course** across all years of the Management Engineering degree curriculum at the University of Pisa.

### 📢 A Call for a Global Community Effort:
To achieve this goal, I invite all students and peers to join this collective initiative:
- **Share your notes:** If you have notes, lecture transcriptions, formula sheets, or solved exam exercises for courses of the second year, third year, or elective modules, please share them! I will gladly review, integrate, and format them in LaTeX so that the entire student community can benefit from them.
- **Spread the word:** Share this repository with your classmates and fellow engineering students so that more people can contribute and freely benefit from these materials.

## 🛠️ Feedback, Improvements and Contributions

Continuous refinement makes these notes better for everyone. If you notice a typo, a missing mathematical step, an unclear diagram, or an error in a proof:

- 🐛 **Open an Issue:** Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Beccaceci/GESTIONALE/issues) to report mistakes, suggest improvements, or request clarifications.
- 🔀 **Submit a Pull Request:** If you want to fix a typo or expand a chapter directly in LaTeX, feel free to fork the repository and open a [Pull Request](https://github.com/Beccaceci/GESTIONALE/pulls).
- ⭐️ **Star the Project:** If you find these notes helpful, leaving a star on GitHub helps make the repository more visible to other students!
